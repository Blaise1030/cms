## Send invitations

Open **Settings → Members** and choose **Invite**. Enter one or more email addresses, separated by commas.

## Choose a role

- **Admin** — manages billing, members and every project.
- **Member** — creates and edits projects.
- **Guest** — sees only the projects shared with them.

You can change a role at any time.
