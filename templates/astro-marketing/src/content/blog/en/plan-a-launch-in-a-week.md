## Monday: decide what "done" means

Write one sentence that describes the launch from the customer's side. Everything that does not serve that sentence moves to next week.

## Tuesday and Wednesday: build the smallest version

Split the work into tasks no bigger than half a day. If a task feels bigger, it is two tasks.

## Thursday: rehearse

Walk through the launch as a customer would: the page, the sign-up, the first email. Fix what trips you up.

## Friday: ship and tell people

Publish, then write a short note about what changed and why. A launch nobody hears about is only a deploy.
