## Why milestones

Projects rarely fail on a single task. They slip when nobody can see how the pieces add up. Milestones give a set of tasks one shared goal and one date, so the whole team can see how close you are.

## How they work

1. Open a project and choose **New milestone**.
2. Give it a name and a target date.
3. Drag tasks onto it, or pick the milestone when you create a task.

Each milestone shows a progress bar based on completed tasks, and turns amber when the date is close and work is still open.

## Available today

Milestones are available on every plan. Read **Organise work with milestones** in the help center for the details.
