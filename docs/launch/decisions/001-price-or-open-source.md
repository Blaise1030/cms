# 001 — Does orso charge, or is it free and open source?

- **Asked by:** Product Engineer
- **Opened:** 2026-11-15
- **Answered:** 2026-11-15, by the user
- **Blocks:** launch gate 6

## Answer

orso is **free and open source under the MIT licence** (`LICENSE` at the repo root). There is no
price, no billing work, and no pricing page.

## Consequences

- `Cargo.toml` and every `package.json` declare `license = "MIT"` / `"license": "MIT"`.
- The Marketer's landing page says "free and open source (MIT)" instead of a pricing section.
- Any future paid offering (hosting, support) is a new decision, not a change to this one.
