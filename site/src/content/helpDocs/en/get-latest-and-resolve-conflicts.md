When other people publish to the same site, your change can fall behind `main`. **Get latest** brings their published work into your change.

## Get latest

Choose **Get latest**. orso pulls in everything published to `main` since you started your change. Most of the time this happens without any questions.

Do this before you [share a preview](/help/share-a-preview/), and before you [publish](/help/publish-your-changes/), so you are testing your change against the current site.

## Conflicts

If someone else edited the same lines you did, orso can't decide which version to keep. It shows you the conflict so you can choose.

- **Choose a version** if one side is clearly right.
- **Hand it to the assistant** if both sides need to be combined. orso can pass the conflict to the agent to resolve.

After the conflict is resolved, check the preview, then carry on.

## Avoiding conflicts

- Keep changes small and publish them often.
- Let teammates know when you are reworking a page that others edit too.
