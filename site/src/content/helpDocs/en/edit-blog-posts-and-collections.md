The **Content** tab lists your site's content collections, such as blog posts, blog categories and help articles.

## Edit an entry

1. Open the **Content** tab and choose a collection.
2. Choose an entry. Its fields appear as a form, and its body opens in the rich-text editor.
3. Make your edits. The preview updates as you go.

If you haven't started a change yet, orso asks you to start one first.

## Fields

The form is built from the collection's schema, so each field gets the right kind of input:

- **Text** for titles and descriptions
- **Dates** for publish dates
- **References** for fields that point to another entry, such as a post's categories
- **Lists** for things like tags
- **Images** for covers and other pictures

Required fields are marked, and orso tells you if a value doesn't fit the schema.

## The body

The body is Markdown, edited in a rich-text editor. Use it for headings, lists, links, quotes, code and images. Headings become the "On this page" links on help articles and the table of contents on blog posts.

## Create or delete an entry

Use the collection's controls to add a new entry or delete one. The entry's ID is its file name, and it becomes part of the page's URL, so choose a short, readable one.

## Read-only collections

A collection marked read-only isn't loaded with `orsoGlob`. You can still view it, and you can ask the assistant to edit its files. See **Make your content collections editable**.
