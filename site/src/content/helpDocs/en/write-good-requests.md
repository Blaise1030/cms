The assistant works best when it knows what to change, where, and what "done" looks like. When it's finished, [review what it changed](../review-agent-changes/).

## Be specific

| Instead of | Try |
| --- | --- |
| "Make the site better" | "Shorten the home page headline to under eight words and make it friendlier" |
| "Add FAQs" | "Add an FAQ section to the pricing page with five questions about billing and refunds" |
| "Fix the colours" | "The buttons on the contact page are hard to read in dark mode. Increase their contrast" |

## Tips

- **Name the page or section.** "The pricing page" or "the footer" saves the agent guessing.
- **One request at a time.** Ask for the next thing after the first one is done.
- **Point at it.** Open the page in the preview first, so you are both looking at the same thing.
- **Give it the words.** If you know the exact copy you want, paste it in.
- **Say what to leave alone.** For example, "don't change the layout, only the text".

## Example requests

- "Write a blog post announcing our new pricing, in the same tone as the last post."
- "Add a testimonials section below the features on the home page with three quotes."
- "Add a help article explaining how to reset a password."
- "Find any broken links on the site and fix them."
