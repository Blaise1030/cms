If none of the troubleshooting articles help, open an issue on GitHub at github.com/Blaise1030/cms/issues.

## What to include

- Your orso version, from `orso --version`.
- Your operating system, such as macOS 15 or Ubuntu 24.04.
- What you clicked or typed, step by step.
- What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
- Any error message, copied as text. Screenshots help too.

Before posting logs or screenshots, remove any tokens, passwords or private details.
