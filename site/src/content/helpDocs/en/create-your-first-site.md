The quickest way to start is the built-in marketing template. It comes with a home page, a blog, a help centre and a deploy workflow, all set up for orso.

## Create the project

1. Start orso with `orso serve --open` and [sign in with GitHub](../sign-in-with-github/).
2. Choose **New project**.
3. Pick the Astro marketing template and give your site a name.

orso creates the repo on GitHub, installs the site's dependencies and starts the live preview. The first time can take a minute while it downloads Node.

## What you get

- **Pages**: a landing page, a blog with categories, a help centre and legal pages.
- **Content collections**: blog posts and help articles you can edit in the **Content** tab. See [Edit blog posts and other collections](../edit-blog-posts-and-collections/).
- **Design settings** in `DESIGN.md`, editable in the **Design** tab. See [Change colours and fonts](../change-colours-and-fonts/).
- **A deploy workflow** that publishes to Cloudflare Workers and gives every pull request its own preview link.
