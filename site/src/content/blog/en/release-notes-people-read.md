## Start with the outcome

"You can now export reports as PDF" beats "We added a new export pipeline". Readers want to know what they can do, not how you built it.

## One change per paragraph

Short paragraphs are easy to skim. If a change needs more than three sentences, link to a help article instead.

## Show it

A single screenshot or short clip answers most questions before they are asked.
