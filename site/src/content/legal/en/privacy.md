This policy explains what personal information Project Orso collects when you use this website and the Project Orso product, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

- **Account details** such as your name and email address when you sign up.
- **Content you add**, including projects, tasks and comments.
- **Usage information** such as pages visited and the browser you use, collected to keep the service working.

## How we use it

We use this information to run the service, answer support requests, send the emails you have asked for and improve the product. We do not sell personal information.

## Your choices

You can update or delete your account at any time from your settings. To ask for a copy of your data, or to ask a question about this policy, email privacy@example.com.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date above, and we tell account holders by email about any significant change.
