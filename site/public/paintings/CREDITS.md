# Painted backdrops

Public-domain Renaissance landscapes used behind the product previews on the home page. Every
image is a landscape with no figures; two are cropped details of larger works so the staffage is
out of frame. Each file is a resized, WebP-compressed copy of the Wikimedia Commons original.

| File | Work | Artist | Date | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `altdorfer-danube.webp` | Danube Landscape with Wörth Castle | Albrecht Altdorfer | c. 1528–30 | [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Landscape_of_Danube_near_Regensburg,_Albrecht_Altdorfer,_W.A.F._30,_Alte_Pinakothek_Munich.jpg) |
| `altdorfer-bridge.webp` | Landscape with a Footbridge (detail) | Albrecht Altdorfer | 1516 | [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Albrecht_Altdorfer_-_Landscape_with_a_Footbridge_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg) |
| `elgreco-toledo.webp` | View of Toledo | El Greco | c. 1598–99 | [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:View_of_Toledo_MET_DP349564.jpg) |
| `verhaecht-alps.webp` | Alpine Landscape (detail) | Tobias Verhaecht | c. 1600 | [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tobias_Verhaecht_Alpine_Landscape_Detail_3.jpg) |
| `valckenborch-rocks.webp` | Mountainous Landscape (detail) | Lucas van Valckenborch | 1582 | [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lucas_van_Valckenborch_-_Mountainous_landscape.jpg) |

All five works are in the public domain, and the Commons files are tagged PD-Art (faithful
reproductions of two-dimensional public-domain works). The source is reduced to 1200px wide and
converted to WebP for use as a backdrop.
