# Painted backdrops

Public-domain Renaissance paintings used behind the product previews on the home page. Each file
is a resized, WebP-compressed copy of the Wikimedia Commons original.

| File | Work | Artist | Date | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `patinir.webp` | Landscape with the Flight into Egypt | Joachim Patinir | c. 1516–17 | [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Joachim_Patinir_-_Landscape_with_the_Flight_into_Egypt_-_WGA17094.jpg) |
| `bruegel.webp` | The Harvesters | Pieter Bruegel the Elder | 1565 | [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pieter_Bruegel_the_Elder-_The_Harvesters_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg) |
| `giorgione.webp` | The Tempest | Giorgione | c. 1506–08 | [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Giorgione,_The_tempest.jpg) |
| `altdorfer.webp` | Danube Landscape | Albrecht Altdorfer | c. 1520–25 | [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Albrecht_Altdorfer_007.jpg) |
| `leonardo.webp` | The Virgin of the Rocks | Leonardo da Vinci | c. 1491–1508 | [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Leonardo_da_Vinci_Virgin_of_the_Rocks_(National_Gallery_London).jpg) |

All five works are in the public domain, and the Commons files are tagged PD-Art (faithful
reproductions of two-dimensional public-domain works). The source dimension is reduced to 1200px
on the long edge and converted to WebP for use as a backdrop.
